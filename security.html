PHASER

Security

Your procedures and design documents are among the most sensitive things your company owns. Phaser is built to keep them yours, and we would rather show you the evidence than ask you to take our word for it.

Your company's data is kept apart

Phaser keeps each company's data apart from every other company's. The database itself enforces that separation, on every table that holds customer data, so it does not depend on each part of the application remembering to check.

We test it by trying to break it

An automated test suite acts as one company and tries to read, change, delete and download another company's work, through the same web endpoints the product itself uses. Every attempt has to be refused. Each refusal is paired with proof that the data was really there to protect, so a test cannot pass by accident.

The tests run on a test copy of Phaser with made-up companies, never on customer data.

Single sign-on with your company accounts

Your people sign in through single sign-on with their company accounts, and Phaser never sees or stores a password. Personal accounts and people your administrator has not invited are turned away. Expired or altered sessions are rejected.

Where your data is processed, when Keramas staff can open it and how long it is kept are on the privacy page.

See the report in a demo

The test suite writes a plain-English report. It lists each check and whether it passed, names the exact version of the software that was tested, and says what it does not cover. Ask for a demo and we will walk you and your security team through it.
